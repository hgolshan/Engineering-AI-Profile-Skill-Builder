How to Use This Profile Builder in Your Engineering Workflow

STEP 01

1. Configure Your Professional Persona

Specify your discipline, experience level, plant type, and key engineering software tools. This ensures the AI understands your technical vocabulary and expected engineering deliverable standards.

STEP 02

2. Select Professional & Discipline Skill Modules

Choose relevant communication, calculation, drafting, and discipline-specific technical rules. Each module injects proven, real-world EPC guidelines into your instructions.

STEP 03

3. Calibrate Rigor & Anti-Hallucination Guardrails

Turn on strict citation rules, calculation displays, and assumption separation. This prevents AI chatbots from fabricating phantom standard clauses or making unstated engineering leaps.

STEP 04

4. Paste into Your AI Assistant

Copy the generated prompt into ChatGPT 'Custom Instructions', Claude 'Project System Prompt', Microsoft Copilot, or your internal enterprise LLM. Save or export your JSON profile for future project reuse.
